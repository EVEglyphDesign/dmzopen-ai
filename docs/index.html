§ 2 · Consent-first

The digital twin

A digital twin is not a copy of your files. It is a working model of your knowledge, judgment, and voice — measured from several independent directions until it locks into place. Fill three vectors and the point in the middle — you — comes into focus.

Identity

Where you come from

Lineage, story, the substrate that makes the model you and no one else.

identity/
Capability

What you can do

The skills, domains, and judgment — what you know how to build, decide, and weigh.

capability/
Values

How you decide

The principles and ethical frame under which you choose what is worth doing.

values/

Three bearings cross and the twin sharpens with every file. Consent-first, forkable, yours to keep, share, or delete. Start with the 30-minute on-ramp.

§ 4

How to submit

For each submission, drop the following into your folder. Incomplete submissions are rejected back to author — never partial-accepted.

  1. 01

    manifest.json

    Copy from manifests/_template.json. Fill every field.

  2. 02

    README.md

    One-page narrative: what you built, the stack used, the surfaces hit, the locks honored.

  3. 03

    /src/

    Source materials — code, prompts, chains, configs.

  4. 04

    /evidence/

    Screenshots, transcripts, dispatch logs, watermarked artifacts.

  5. 05

    /revert.md

    Exact procedure to undo your build. REVERSIBILITY-00 applies.

  6. 06

    /sha256.txt

    SHA-256 of every file you submitted, sorted. The manifest must match.

Every artifact must carry the EVEGlyph watermark before submission. See the mandate below, or the canonical spec at watermark/PROTOCOL.md.

§ 5 · The mandate

Binding canon

Everything under this umbrella acknowledges the following locks. Removal or alteration of watermarks is treated as a copyright-violation event under ONE-UMBRELLA-00 (pending counsel).

NO-PSYCH-00

No operator psychology or physical-state material is collected, accepted, or routed through this surface.

REVERSIBILITY-00

Every change must be revertable. A submission without a clean revert procedure is incomplete.

CONSENT-FIRST-00

Digital twins and data are contributed by you, about you. Nothing is scraped or pre-collected. You own it.

COPYRIGHT-00

Submissions enter the umbrella per ONE-UMBRELLA-00 (pending counsel).

HUMAN-FAVOR-00

Operator authority is unimpaired. The human is the constraint. Buttons present before the loop closes.

PROPOSE-ONLY

Submitting ≠ accepted. Apex review gates merge. Until certified, status is submitted-pending-apex.

APEX-CERT-00

Operator certifies. No autonomous merge to the umbrella record.

CHANNEL-LOCK

OneDrive is forbidden. Local-first to Downloads; watermarked-image-via-WhatsApp confirms physical storage.

PATENT-GRACE

Patent grace clock 2027-05-17 · US §102(b)(1)(A). Treat all submitted material as patent-grace-clock IP.

§ 6

After submission

Every action against this channel is logged, append-only, to the chain-of-custody ledger. Below is the path from receipt to counsel handoff.

  1. 1

    Intake check

    Manifest schema validation, watermark presence per artifact type, SHA-256 verification. Logged to chain-of-custody/<stack>-<utc>.md.

  2. 2

    Conformance scoring

    Submission graded against the canon rubric in eve-glyph-archive/canon/_pending-counsel/HCP-CONSOLIDATION-MATRIX.pdf.

  3. 3

    Apex review

    Operator certifies per APEX-CERT-00. Until certified, status is submitted-pending-apex.

  4. 4

    Counsel routing

    Full bench result PDF goes to copyright counsel via _pending-counsel/ staging.
